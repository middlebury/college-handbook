title: B.5.g. Time Limit for Completion of Degree
date: 09/03/2026
---
For students in MIO programs that have successfully completed 80% of the program by end of Fall 2026 term, MIO will make special arrangements for remaining classes needed to complete the degree by end of Summer 2027 term.
